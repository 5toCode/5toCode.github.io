Welcome to the new `/writing/` section. This site was just migrated from flat HTML to [Astro](https://astro.build), and this is the first post.

## What changed

- The portfolio is now built with **Astro 6** and **Tailwind CSS v4**.
- The TrainIQ and StrengthIQ app pages live at the same URLs as before.
- There's a new `/writing/` section (you're reading it) with an RSS feed at `/rss.xml`.

## Why Astro

Astro generates zero-JS static HTML by default, ships only the JavaScript you explicitly add, and has excellent support for markdown-based content through Content Collections. For a portfolio site that mostly needs fast static pages with an occasional bit of interactivity (the hamburger nav), it's a great fit.
